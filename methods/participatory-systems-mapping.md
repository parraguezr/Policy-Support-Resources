Participants jointly construct a map of factors and relationships, then use it to discuss how a complex system works and where interventions might matter. UNDP describes iterative workshops and subsequent map analysis.[^s1]

## How to use

Agree scope, elicit factors and relationships, discuss link direction and uncertainty, then review patterns and possible leverage points. Retain who contributed a claim, disagreement and workshop context alongside the map.

## Evidence and limitations

Maps represent participating groups' knowledge and beliefs. Missing voices and facilitation choices affect the result. Network centrality is not itself a causal effect or an intervention priority.

## Suggested project use

Pair extracted document relationships with workshop annotation in [UNDP sensemaking](../projects/undp-sensemaking.md). For MinCiencia, explore differing views on research ecosystem barriers without merging all views into one asserted truth.
