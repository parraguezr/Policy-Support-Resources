Smarter Crowdsourcing combines a focused problem with deliberately recruited expertise and structured deliberation, turning insights into actionable proposals. Expertise includes practical experience as well as formal credentials.[^s1]

## Workflow

Define actionable problems; identify relevant expertise; convene moderated discussions; develop proposals with implementation needs, timelines and measures.

## Evidence and limitations

The method's own description establishes the process, not the effectiveness of every application. Participant selection, inclusion and how proposals are adopted remain important evaluation questions.

## Suggested project use

A workshop and expert-engagement complement to AI synthesis. MinCiencia's ecosystem discovery could inform who is invited; UNDP's source exploration could support the problem brief.
