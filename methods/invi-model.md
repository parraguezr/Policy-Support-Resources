INVI describes an AI-ethnographic approach that processes large-scale qualitative input from citizens, practitioners and stakeholders. It combines computational organization with interpretation of collective knowledge.[^s1]

The model discusses wickedness across causes, solutions, scale and conflict. Its numerical dimensions should be understood within the provider's methodology; they are not causal impact estimates.

## Suggested project use

Explore how interviews and workshop contributions can remain traceable while surfacing clusters and disagreement. For both projects, separate whose perspective is represented from what an analyst infers.
