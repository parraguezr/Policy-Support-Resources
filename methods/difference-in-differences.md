Difference in differences compares the before-to-after change for a treated group with the corresponding change for a comparison group. UNDP describes it as a quasi-experimental evaluation approach.[^s1]

## What it needs

An intervention with a defensible treatment definition, comparable outcome measurements over time, and a credible comparison group. The key identifying assumption is that, without treatment, the groups would have followed parallel trends.

## Evidence and limitations

Baseline imbalance, different trends, spillovers and concurrent shocks can undermine the estimate. A before/after chart alone is insufficient. An observational dashboard does not automatically provide an evaluation design.

## Suggested project use

Potentially useful for assessing a future policy or programme when treatment timing and longitudinal data are available. First establish feasibility; neither project homepage demonstrates such a dataset.
