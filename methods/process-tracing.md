Process tracing examines whether the evidence expected under a proposed causal mechanism is present in a case. UNDP describes tests with different evidentiary strength, including hoop and smoking-gun tests.[^s1]

## How to use

Specify a mechanism and credible alternatives; derive observable implications; collect traces from documents, events or interviews; assess how strongly each trace supports or challenges each explanation.

## Evidence and limitations

An abundance of documents is not the same as strong discriminating evidence. Missing records, selective testimony and alternative explanations need explicit treatment.

## Suggested project use

A useful evaluation companion to document sensemaking: connect a proposed mechanism to dated source passages and contradictory evidence. It requires purposeful investigation beyond automated topic extraction.
