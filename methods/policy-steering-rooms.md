Geoff Mulgan and Giulio Quaggiotto propose issue-focused steering rooms organized around four kinds of material: patterns, proven knowledge, promising innovations and possible futures.[^s1]

## Four complementary views

- **Patterns:** facts and trends that frame the situation.
- **Proven:** evidence about what works, including failures.
- **Promising:** innovations worth exploring.
- **Possible:** longer-term possibilities and scenarios.

## Suggested project use

An organizing frame for a workshop or dashboard session. Show qualitative voices and uncertainties alongside indicators, and capture decisions and follow-up learning separately from the displayed evidence.

## Evidence and limitations

This is a design proposal, not proof that a room improves outcomes. The four categories should not flatten differences in evidence quality or time horizon.
