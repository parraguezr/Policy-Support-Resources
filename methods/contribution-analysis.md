Contribution analysis iteratively develops and tests a theory of change against evidence, assumptions and other factors that could explain observed outcomes.[^s1]

## How to use

Set out the contribution question, articulate the causal chain, gather evidence, assess the story's weaknesses, seek additional evidence and revise. Preserve disconfirming material and competing causes.

## Evidence and limitations

The aim is a credible assessment of contribution, not exclusive attribution. Weak evidence at key links remains a limitation even when the overall narrative is persuasive.

## Suggested project use

Structure an evidence matrix for a programme or policy: claimed mechanism, expected observation, supporting source, counterevidence and unresolved question. This can connect UNDP's exploration interface to evaluation practice.
