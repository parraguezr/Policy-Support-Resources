Agent-based modelling simulates entities following rules and interacting with one another and their environment. Macro patterns emerge from these micro-level interactions.[^s1]

## What it needs

Explicit agent types, behavioural rules, interactions, initialization and outcome measures. Calibration, validation and sensitivity analysis matter because different assumptions can produce different futures.

## Evidence and limitations

Simulation explores consequences of assumptions; it does not establish that the assumptions describe reality. Models can require considerable data, expertise and computation. These simulated agents are conceptually distinct from LLM workflow agents.

## Suggested project use

A possible later-stage approach to policy scenarios once a clearly bounded mechanism and calibration evidence exist. Start with the question and model requirements, not with an attractive animation.
