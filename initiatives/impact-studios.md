Impact Studios describes participatory problem-solving that combines AI support with community knowledge and domain expertise. Its process moves from a well-defined problem through expert engagement to implementable proposals.[^s1][^s2]

## Benchmark value

For either project, the useful pattern is the connection between evidence tools and a facilitated decision process: who contributes, how ideas are challenged, and who owns implementation.

## Evidence and limitations

The website and supplied presentation describe a programme and its claimed benefits. They do not independently demonstrate outcomes. Participation quality and implementation follow-through need their own evidence.
