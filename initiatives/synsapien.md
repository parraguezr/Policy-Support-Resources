SynSapien takes a policy question and context, reviews published literature and proposes possible solutions. The supplied examples concern offshore renewables and marine issues, and kerbside EV charging.[^s1]

## Benchmark value

The question → evidence → options sequence is relevant to both projects. Explicit context and source access could help users inspect whether a suggested option fits their setting.

## Evidence and limitations

The site labels the product a beta prototype and advises verification of AI-generated material. Example questions do not imply endorsement by the organizations associated with them. This review inspected examples and descriptions; no new analysis was commissioned or evaluated.
