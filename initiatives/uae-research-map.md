The portal presents a way to connect researchers, institutions, funders and industry through research information and opportunities.[^s1]

## Benchmark value

For [MinCiencia](../projects/minciencia-ctci.md), study how the entry points distinguish actors, resources and opportunities. A national portal needs clear entity identities and useful relationships, not only aggregated counts.

## Evidence and limitations

The supplied homepage and ecosystem view were inspected. Search performance, data completeness, underlying APIs, reuse rights and operational ownership were not independently established. Live counts are intentionally not treated as stable facts.
