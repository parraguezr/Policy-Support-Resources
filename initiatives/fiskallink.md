## What it is

Eryawan Presma Yulianrifat's case study presents FiskalLink AI as a Ministry of Finance project that maps a plain-language policy theme to budget lines through reviewed, traceable analysis.[^fiskallink]

Its visible demonstration stages cover converting a theme into an SOP, carrying reviewer corrections to similar lines, tracing a ledger to headline totals, separating regional and national views, and examining spending destinations.[^fiskallink]

## Evidence and limitations

The page reports 1.83 million reconciled budget records covering USD 224.4 billion, and a turnaround change from five expert days to a 20-minute workflow. These are the developer's reported figures, not independently reproduced results.[^fiskallink]

The interface is labelled an illustrative mockup with sample figures and masked region names. The page distinguishes its reported impact figures from those demo values. No production ledger, operational system or benchmark evidence was examined.[^fiskallink]
