ImpactAI presents policy guidance from randomized evaluations, with source attribution, comparison of standardized effects and interactive evidence exploration.[^s1]

## Benchmark value

Useful for studying how an interface moves from a question to studies and outcomes while keeping the evidence inspectable. This complements broader document sensemaking, whose corpus may include plans and claims rather than impact estimates.

## Evidence and limitations

The landing page describes the product; retrieval accuracy, effect harmonization and external validity were not independently tested. A result from one population or intervention cannot automatically be transported to another.

## Relationships

A product of the World Bank's [Development Impact AI Lab](../organizations/world-bank-development-impact.md).[^s2] Compare [SynSapien](synsapien.md) and [difference in differences](../methods/difference-in-differences.md).
