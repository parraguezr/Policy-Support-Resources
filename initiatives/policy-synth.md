Policy Synth is described by the Burnes Center as a toolkit using AI agents and evolutionary approaches to research problems and develop policy solutions. It is connected to human engagement through Smarter Crowdsourcing.[^s1][^s2]

## Why it matters

It provides a concrete comparison for organizing problem definition, research, idea generation and review into distinct stages. The software is separately represented so the initiative's ambitions are not confused with installable components.

## Evidence and limitations

The live demo describes an early automated test with a human-supplied problem statement.[^s3] The paper reports a bounded comparison, not a causal evaluation of improved public outcomes. Provider case claims need separate verification.

## Relationships

Developed with [Citizens Foundation](../organizations/citizens-foundation.md) and the [Burnes Center](../organizations/burnes-center.md).
