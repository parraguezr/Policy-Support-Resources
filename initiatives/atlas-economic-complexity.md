Harvard Growth Lab's Atlas presents research and trade-data visualizations for understanding countries' economic structures and potential growth opportunities.[^s1]

## Benchmark value

For MinCiencia, it is a reference for linking an overview to an interpretable network and country-specific exploration. The broader design lesson is to explain what nodes, edges, colour and size mean.

## Evidence and limitations

The homepage and supplied Product Space view were inspected. The underlying dataset was not downloaded or validated. Economic capability proximity should not be relabelled as organizational collaboration or causal influence.
