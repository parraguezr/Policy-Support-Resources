CentreAI presents a five-stage workflow: Purpose, Context, Options, Challenge and Choice. Its example connects a policy brief with evidence, options and simulated challenge.[^s1]

## What to learn from it

The explicit stages make the analytical process visible and keep the decision recognizable as a human responsibility. This is useful inspiration for the transition from evidence exploration to policy options in both projects.

## Evidence and limitations

The page identifies the offering as a service, not open-source software. Its homelessness example uses real published evidence, but dialogue, packages and reactions are authored illustrations, not statements by the organizations depicted. No service performance was tested.
