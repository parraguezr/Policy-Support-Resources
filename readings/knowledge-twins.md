The visible article heading is “Knowledge Twins”; its browser title is “Democratic Government and Shared Intelligence.” Geoff Mulgan discusses combining data, evidence, foresight and tacit knowledge so government can reason and learn more effectively.[^s1]

**Suggested use:** an architectural prompt for both projects: distinguish observed data, causal assumptions, futures and stakeholder knowledge, then connect them transparently.

**Limits:** a conceptual proposal rather than a verified deployed benchmark. A knowledge twin should not be conflated with a calibrated digital simulation.
