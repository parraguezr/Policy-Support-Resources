## Summary

The brief uses the Women's Empowerment Principles (WEPs) to connect inclusive pandemic recovery with public policy and private-sector practice. It addresses women's roles as leaders, workers and entrepreneurs (pp. 2–3).[^pdf]

Its three priorities are leadership commitment, equality in workplaces and markets, and accountability through gender reporting (p. 4). Recommended measures include care services, parental leave, leadership development, support for women-owned enterprises, gender-responsive budgeting and procurement (pp. 15–20).[^pdf]

A central theme is comparable, sex-disaggregated business data. The reporting framework covers leadership representation, pay, hiring and promotion, parental-leave retention, and grievance mechanisms, with complementary procurement indicators. ASEAN-level coordination is paired with actions for Member States, regulators and stock exchanges (pp. 17–20).[^pdf]

Country examples and annexes illustrate existing policies, reporting gaps and promising practices, including stock-exchange sustainability reporting (pp. 5–14, 21–25).[^pdf]

## Evidence and limitations

The recommendations and examples reflect the brief's pandemic-recovery context. Statistics and descriptions of national policies were not refreshed or independently verified; recommended measures do not establish demonstrated effectiveness.
