The Strategy Science introduction by Felipe Csaszar, Gwendolyn Lee, Peter Zemsky and Todd Zenger proposes two ladders: the cognitive demands of strategic tasks and the degree of responsibility delegated to AI.[^s1]

The central distinction is useful: stronger analytical capability does not automatically justify greater autonomy. Verifiability, downside and accountability affect delegation choices.

**Suggested use:** classify a prospective feature as analysis, prediction, intervention reasoning or model construction; separately decide what the human must inspect and authorize.

**Evidence:** an introductory framework essay, not a controlled evaluation of either project. DOI: 10.1287/stsc.2026.intro.v11.n1.
