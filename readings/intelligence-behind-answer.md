Manish Srivastava distinguishes what question an analysis answers from how the answer is produced: rules, models, simulation, optimization and language models have different assumptions and evidentiary requirements.[^s1]

**Suggested use:** attach purpose, method, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and decision responsibility to analytical outputs. A human approval checkbox alone does not explain whether an answer deserves trust.

**Evidence:** an analytical essay, not a comparative experiment.
